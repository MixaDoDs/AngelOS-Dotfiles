# Hell's circles: where the sins' sounds come from

Each circle has two (`<circle>-1.ogg`, `<circle>-2.ogg`); one plays by chance over the low blow in
the dark between the circles (services/CircleFx → Sounds.playSin). They are made by
`scripts/hell-sounds.py` from the recordings in `src/`: trimmed, put on a stereo path, given
reverb (and some a ping-pong echo), levelled.

All of them CC0 (public domain): free to use, change and share, no credit needed — given here anyway.
Freesound previews (mp3), turned into .ogg at 48 kHz.
